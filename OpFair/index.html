What We Do

Opportunity Fair has been bridging the gap between Metro Vancouver's technology industry and SFU's passionate engineering students since 1993, fostering professional connections between developers of today and tomorrow.

Over the years, we have linked 500+ students with 100+ companies. OpFair is hosted annually at Simon Fraser University's Burnaby Campus.

Follow OpFair on LinkedIn →

Hosted by the Engineering Science Student Society

The Engineering Science Student Society (ESSS) is established to serve and represent the interests of its members to the School of Engineering Science, throughout Simon Fraser University, and beyond to industry and other engineering societies.
