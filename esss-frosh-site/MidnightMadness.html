Midnight Madness🎉

Date Friday, September 18 through Saturday, September 19
Time 8:00 PM to 8:00 AM
Location Applied Sciences Building SFU Burnaby Campus

Hosted by: ESSS × MSESS × SEESS

🎉 Midnight Madness is the BIGGEST Frosh Week event, held in collaboration between all three SFU engineering programs: Engineering Science from Burnaby, and Sustainable Energy Engineering and Mechatronic Systems Engineering from Surrey!

With 200+ students coming together throughout the night, we're bringing all three engineering communities together for one huge overnight event packed with competitions, activities, and prizes. Expect design challenges, scavenger hunts, karaoke, Capture the Flag, and more, with chances to win big prizes like Apple products, gaming gear, Raspberry Pis, and more.

There will be free pizza, snacks, and drinks supplied throughout the event, and a complimentary breakfast if you make it until morning. Midnight Madness is the ultimate opportunity to meet your future friends and make unforgettable memories. 🎉
